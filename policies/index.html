Care Guide

  • Take pieces off before showering, swimming, working out, or sleeping.
  • Put on perfume, lotion, and hairspray first, then your jewelry.
  • Wipe with a soft, dry cloth after wearing. Use mild soap and warm water if needed, then dry fully.
  • Store each piece separately in a pouch or box so chains and stones don’t scratch each other.
  • Avoid harsh chemicals, chlorine, and abrasive cleaners.
